How to read these rankings
  • Relevance score (0–100) shows how closely a paper matches your exact question: the population, the exposure or treatment, and the outcome. It is not a measure of study quality or of whether a treatment works.
  • Direct papers match the key elements of your question. Related papers match an important part but differ in population, intervention or outcome. Background papers give context only.
  • Confidence is how sure the AI is about its relevance rating. It is lower when the abstract is short or missing.
  • Used in answer marks the papers the written answer draws on. Protocols without results and papers with a low relevance score are left out of the answer, though you can still read them here.
  • All assessments use titles and abstracts only. Check the full paper before relying on a result.
